# Product Mission

## Pitch
Reader3 is an AI-assisted reading companion that helps returning readers and deep learners stay engaged with and retain dense books by providing chapter-level AI overlays, synced TTS, and guided voice reflections.

## Users

### Primary Customers
- Habit rebuilders: Readers restarting their reading habit and needing low-friction ways to stay engaged.
- Deep study readers: People tackling dense, technical, or academic texts who want structured comprehension and reflection support.

### User Personas
**Habit Rebuilder** (25-45)
- **Role:** Working professional balancing limited time.
- **Context:** Reads on desktop or mobile and wants to resume a stalled reading habit.
- **Pain Points:** Loses focus, forgets progress, struggles to re-engage after breaks.
- **Goals:** Finish more books, keep momentum, and enjoy the process.

**Deep Study Reader** (30-50)
- **Role:** Researcher, analyst, or student reading dense material.
- **Context:** Works through complex books chapter by chapter with note-taking.
- **Pain Points:** Cognitive overload, shallow retention, limited time for reflection.
- **Goals:** Extract key ideas quickly, build durable understanding, and connect concepts.

## The Problem

### Passive reading stalls engagement and retention
Without guidance or multimodal support, readers lose focus and abandon books; dense chapters are slow to parse, leading to drop-off and weak recall.

**Our Solution:** Combine AI overlays, synced TTS highlighting, and guided voice reflections to keep readers engaged, clarify dense sections, and reinforce learning.

## Differentiators

### Integrated multimodal guidance
Unlike generic e-readers or standalone TTS apps, we pair text, synced audio, and chapter-aware AI overlays in one flow, improving completion and recall.

### Context-aware reflection
Unlike generic chatbots, reflections pull full book and chapter context to ask targeted questions, helping readers form insights they remember.

## Key Features

### Core Features
- **Chapter-level AI overlays:** Explain, summarize, mind map, and Q&A per chapter so readers grasp the essentials quickly.
- **Synced TTS with highlights:** Play audio via your TTS API while highlighting text to keep eyes and ears aligned and reduce drop-offs.

### Collaboration Features
- **Shareable reflections and notes:** Export summaries or reflection prompts so readers can discuss or revisit key takeaways with peers.
- **Session replays:** Revisit prior reflection sessions to track progress and share insights asynchronously.

### Advanced Features
- **Voice-driven reflection sessions:** Conversational AI asks thoughtful, context-aware questions to deepen understanding.
- **Mobile-friendly, hostable experience:** Self-hosted deployment optimized for desktop and mobile so readers can continue on the go.
